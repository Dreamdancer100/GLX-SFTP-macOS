## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **Two panes:** Mac files, server files or terminal on each side, with swap and ready-made views.
- 🧩 **Drag from Finder:** Drag files between Mac and server, also straight from Finder.
- ⚙️ **Copy, cut and paste:** On the server and on your Mac with Cmd+C, Cmd+X and Cmd+V; pasting into the other pane transfers the files.
- 🔒 **Never overwrites by accident:** Existing names get a copy suffix; deleting on the Mac moves files to the Trash.
- 📂 **SFTP, SSH, FTP and FTPS:** Explicit and implicit FTPS on port 990, SSH terminal included.
- ⚡ **Import your servers:** From FileZilla, Cyberduck, OpenSSH config and exports of PuTTY, KiTTY, MobaXterm and WinSCP; save them back for those programs.
- 🎨 **Favourites with icons:** Folders, 300+ included icons and your own icon libraries.
- 🌍 **Mac shortcuts:** Cmd+N new connection, Cmd+K quick connect, Cmd+W disconnect, Cmd+Backspace delete.
- 🛠️ **Native Mac app:** Intel and Apple silicon, Mac window buttons, runs once and brings the open window to the front.
- 📊 **12 designs, two languages:** German and English, built-in help, 14-day trial.
